05 · WHAT YOU CAN DO TODAY — JUST CHATGPT

five beginner moves. one tool. zero new logins.

01
BRIEF — write the prompt like a contract."you are [role]. context: 3 lines about my situation. constraint: 1 line. write 3 versions in different tones."
02
PASTE — use your real work, not toy examples.paste the actual email, doc, deck draft. ask: what’s missing? what’s weak? what would a skeptic say?
03
STEAL — reverse-engineer something good.paste a competitor’s landing page or email. ask: what’s the structure? now rewrite it for my topic.
04
TEACH-BACK — the cheat code nobody uses.after any complex answer: "explain that back to me like i’m in 5th grade." if it can’t, you didn’t understand it.
05
SAVE — start a "prompts that worked" doc.one google doc. paste the winners in. that’s your skill set — written down. six months from now, the most valuable doc in your career.
06 · WHAT TO ASK — REAL PROMPTS, REAL ROLES

you’re not stuck on the tool.
you’re stuck on the question.

SALES
"draft 5 cold openers for the CFO of a 50-person SaaS. one line each. tones: blunt, curious, helpful, contrarian, transactional."90 seconds in. you’ve just done a junior SDR’s morning of work.
MARKETING
"review this landing page. flag 3 trust-killers. propose a one-sentence fix for each."a $0 teardown instead of a $5K conversion audit.
OPS
"i run weekly status meetings. give me a notion template that captures decisions, blockers, owner, deadline."your meeting cadence, standardized in one prompt.
EXEC ASST
"summarize this 30-page PDF into a 1-page briefing for my boss. cut the fluff. surface the trade-offs."you’ve just bought your boss an hour back.
HR
"rewrite this JD for senior engineers. less corporate. honest about the team’s actual trade-offs."attract the people who’d thrive. repel the rest. faster.
07 · THE OPERATOR TRAJECTORY

five capabilities. compound in order.

01
FRAME — write the brief like a contract, not a request.context, constraint, format, deadline. if your prompt is shorter than your last slack to a junior, you’re doing it wrong.
02
ROUTE — pick the right door.chat for the phone. cowork for daily ops. claude code for swarms. wrong tool = wrong answer.
03
REMEMBER — hand the worker the journal every morning.a folder of markdown files. the model is amnesiac; the vault is your continuity.
04
PARALLELIZE — spawn the swarm in one message.ten agents sequential = 100 minutes. ten agents parallel = 10 minutes. same model, ten times the speed.
05
ENCODE — when you’ve explained it three times, it becomes a skill.skills are the difference between using AI and operating AI.
08 · HOW THE GENERALIST ACTUALLY LEARNS

one weekend. one shipped artifact. repeat.

FRIDAY NIGHT
pick a real problem
from your actual job. not a toy. write it on a sticky note.
→
SATURDAY
build the ugly version
whatever tool fits. v0, cursor, n8n, obsidian. ugly is fine. broken is fine. shipped is the bar.
→
SUNDAY
show it to one human
your manager. a friend. someone on X. note what broke. fix it or kill it.

twelve weekends = twelve shipped artifacts = a portfolio.  a portfolio outranks a resume every single time in 2026. i wrote a 44-chapter book this way while running five companies.
