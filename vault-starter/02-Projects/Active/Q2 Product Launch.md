engagements; this packages our top-quartile workflow as a fixed-price
monthly plan. Three tiers ($3K / $8K / $20K).

## Why this exists
- Custom engagements cap our growth (each new client adds ops overhead)
- Mid-market wants predictable pricing, not custom
- We have 18 months of internal playbook data — it's productizable
- Margin uplift estimated at 1.6× on equivalent customer count
