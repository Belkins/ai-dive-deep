## Why this works

1. **Voice survives.** Every hype title moves into the subtitle slot. The chapter page still opens with "The Day I Killed My Tabs" as the evocative line.
2. **Cmd-K becomes precise.** Type "cron" → matches Ch 07 immediately. Type "browser" → Ch 33. Type "tier" → Ch 24. The old titles required reading 36 metaphors.
3. **Scanning the chapter grid drops from ~6 sec to ~2 sec** because the eye lands on subjects, not stories.
4. **SEO improves.** Every `<title>` tag is now a literal topic phrase ("Permissions and Sandboxes — Ultimate AI Dive Deep") instead of a metaphor.
