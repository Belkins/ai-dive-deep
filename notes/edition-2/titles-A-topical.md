# Title system A — Topical / Clean

## Philosophy

Lead with the subject in 2-5 words so a reader scanning Cmd-K can locate the chapter in under a second. Treat the title as a textbook heading, not a story hook — voice still shows through receipt numbers, em-dashes, and lowercase tendencies where they sharpen meaning. Reserve narrative phrasing for subtitles; titles must survive being read out of order.

## Three you're least sure about

- **Ch 03 — "Stateless Instances, Durable Artifacts"**: technically precise but loses the temp-agency analogy that actually makes the mental model stick. The topical version reads like a database paper; a reader scanning Cmd-K for "why does Claude forget" won't pattern-match on "stateless."
- **Ch 10 — "Hosted Agents, Local Models, Frontier"**: comma-separated taxonomy is honest about the contents but it's three topics, not one. Violates the "instantly know what chapter this is" test because no single subject leads.
- **Ch 17 — "25 Operator Tips"**: receipt number is sharp, but "tips" is the exact pure-list framing the constraint warns against being repetitive. If Ch 28 lands as "Six Failures, Six Bills" the parallel numeric construction starts to feel like a tic across the TOC.
